How each zone is decided

TrimA single month's inflow above 15% of Gold ETF AUM, a crowd frenzy. Sell 25%. Weak evidence: it fired only twice since 2012 (Feb 2020, Jan 2026). Checked first.

HoldFlow percentile at 30 or below and Gold BeES below its 10-month average. Park the gold SIP in a liquid fund until either turns.

Accumulate moreFlow percentile at 60 or above. Keep the SIP and invest half of any cash parked during Hold.

AccumulateEverything else. Keep the normal SIP.

Each month's zone uses the previous month's data, because AMFI publishes flows around the 10th of the next month. Heavy inflows mean "accumulate", not "trim": from 2011 to 2026 gold did better after crowded months than after quiet ones.

Data, method and limits
  • Flows and AUM: AMFI monthly reports, Gold ETF row, from Feb 2009. June 2014 was derived from AMFI's fiscal-year-to-date totals.
  • Price: Gold BeES NAV from AMFI (via mfapi.in), chained across the scheme's three AMFI codes and its 2019 unit split. The current month shows the latest daily NAV.
  • Flow percentile: 3-month net inflow divided by AUM three months earlier, ranked within the trailing 36 months (needs 24 months of history).
  • A GitHub Action refreshes the data every weekday evening. About 17 years of flow data cover roughly one and a half sentiment cycles, so treat the zones as a tested guide, not a guarantee. Not investment advice.
