Frenzy warningA single month's inflow above 15% of Gold ETF AUM. Consider trimming gold back to your target weight. It fired only twice since 2012 (Feb 2020, Jan 2026), so it is a warning, not a zone.

Each month uses the previous month's data, because AMFI publishes flows around the 10th of the next month. The price check uses month-end closes. Heavy inflows mean "accumulate", not "trim": from 2011 to 2026 gold did better after crowded months than after quiet ones.

Data, method and limits
  • Flows and AUM: AMFI monthly reports, Gold ETF row, from Feb 2009. June 2014 was derived from AMFI's fiscal-year-to-date totals.
  • Price: Gold BeES NAV from AMFI (via mfapi.in), chained across the scheme's three AMFI codes and its 2019 unit split.
  • Flow percentile: 3-month net inflow divided by AUM three months earlier, ranked within the trailing 36 months (needs 24 months of history).
  • Month-end line: the 10-month average includes the month's own close, so the price stays in a downtrend if it closes below the average of the previous nine month-ends.
  • Rules tested and rejected: buying when flows are quiet and trimming when crowded (8.3% a year vs 14.0% for the SIP; lost in all 108 settings tried), and pausing on quiet flows alone (higher return, 14.6%, but 19% of money idle on average).
  • Silver BeES is not covered: AMFI has reported Silver ETFs on their own row only since Aug 2026, far too short for a 3-year percentile.
  • A GitHub Action refreshes the data every weekday evening and opens a GitHub issue (emailed to the owner) when the zone changes or new AMFI data arrives. About 17 years of flow data cover roughly one and a half sentiment cycles. A tested guide, not investment advice.
